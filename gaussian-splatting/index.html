Demo site Knoll Survey, a synthetic 120 × 120 m knoll flown by drone

Designed by Prof. Xin (Shane) Miao, Missouri State University. Part of Computer Graphics Explorables, an open set of interactive teaching modules. Everything on this page runs locally in your browser; three.js draws the Gradient Lab scene and MathJax typesets the mathematics. Method: Kerbl, Kopanas, Leimkühler & Drettakis, 3D Gaussian Splatting for Real-Time Radiance Field Rendering, SIGGRAPH 2023; EWA splatting after Zwicker et al. (2002); SfM after Schönberger & Frahm (2016). Full references are on the Lab page. MIT licensed.
